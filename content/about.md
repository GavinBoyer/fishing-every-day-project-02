# About Fishing Every Day

I created this databse as a final project for my Fundamentals of Information Organization class at Texas Tech University. I have since fallen in love with the site. The amount of hours, blood, sweat, and tears that I have put into this database makes it special to me. My new purpose with this project is to educate current and future generations about fishing, and to encourage more people to step out of their comfort zones and try something they never have before.

## About Myself

My name is Gavin, and I am a senior Computing Applications major at Texas Tech University. I am an avid outdoorsman and have been all of my life. From camping with my family as a kid to getting out nearly every weekend to get some [line](fishing-line/index.md) wet. I love the outdoors. Even if I am not fishing, I am usually outside whether that be hiking, or simply sitting on my backporch enjoying the weather. I was part of the Scouts of America which truly drew out my love for nature. I strive to do everything in my power to help aid in environmental conservation and I am an avid believer in "leave no trace." I recently relocated to San Antonio with my loving partner and our cat Yo-Yo. Adjusting to thr weather was rough, but the fishing made up for it.

## Take Action

I urge you to take a step outside today. Take a walk. Ride your bike. Sit on the porch. ==Just go outside.== While you're out there, you might as well pick up a piece of trash or two and do your part to help conserve our planet.
