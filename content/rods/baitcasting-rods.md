# Baitcasting Rods
### What is a Baitcasting Rod?

Baitcasting rods are traditionally an intermediate rod. They are designed specifically for baitcasting reels and to cast your lures more accurately. Baitcasting rods are mostly used for bass fishing, and many anglers prefer them over [[spinning-rods]] as they offer more power and tend to be more stiff. Baitcasting rods are also referred to as casting rods, but using two names can become confusing, so this site refers to  them as baitcasting rods.

>A baitcasting rod is a specialized fishing rod designed for use with a baitcasting reel. This type of rod is typically characterized by its sturdy construction, which allows greater power and precision when casting. The reel is mounted on top of the rod, enabling angler to control the line more effectively.

#### Significant Features of Baitcasting Rods

- **Reel Placement**: On baitcasting rods, the reel is placed on top of the rod, unlike [[fly-rods]], and offers a more direct path for the line. This also increases casting accuracy and control.
- **Guides**: Guides are what keeps the line on the rod itself, on a baitcasting rod they are on the top side of the rod, similar to [[spincast-rods]], allowing it to bend with ease. It also helps to reduce tangles and eases the cast.
- **Power and Action**: Like most rods, baitcasting rods are offered in a variety or power rating which range from light to heavy. The are also offered in several different actions. The action refers to how much the rod tips bends with pressure. Light action being the rod bends near the tip, and slow action being the rod bending throughout the entire rod. These rods tend to be heavier and less sensitive than [[surf-rods]]
- **Construction Materials**: The majority of baitcasting rods are made from high-quality materials, including but not limited to, graphite, fiberglass, or a combination. Graphite rods tend to be lighter and more fragile while fiberglass rods have better strength and durability.
