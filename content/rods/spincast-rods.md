# Spincast Rods
### What is a Spincast Rod?

Spincast rods are traditionally a beginner's fishing rod. They normally come in combos which means it comes with the rod and reel unlike a lot of [[surf-rods]]. These rods have a casting or "push-button" reel, that has a closed-face and allows for easy casting and retrieving as a beginner. A lot of spincast rods tend to be small children's rods, however, Zebco push button combos remain a staple even in modern day fishing. Spincast rods are great for actually casting the bait. While most people use spincast rods for children, a lot of anglers prefer the ease and simplicity over [[fly-rods]]. 

>You reach into your pack for your fishing gear, but you do not want to spend the next twenty minutes untangling a "bird's nest" from a complicated baitcasting reel. You need something that works every time with zero fuss. This is where the spincast rod comes into play. While often dismissed as a tool for children, a high-quality spincast setup is a reliable workhorse for hikers, campers, and survivalists alike.

#### Significant Features of Casting Rods

- **Reel Placement**: Similar to [[baitcasting-rods]], spincast rods have the reel placed on top of the rod to make it easier to push the button. This also helps to make casting easier and produce farther casts.
- **Guides**: Spincast rods have their guides placed on top to align with the reel and to make for a more efficient and smooth cast.
- **Power and Action**: Spincast rods, while versatile, are not offered in as many powers and actions as [[spinning-rods]], since the spincast reel does limit the rod. Much like the rest of the rods, these are offered in actions ranging from light to heavy and lengths ranging from 3ft all the way up to 7 feet. They tend to stay in the shorter range as they are more meant for smaller fish, but the longer ones are capable of handling larger fish.
- **Construction Materials**: Most modern day spincast rods are made with a combination of graphite and fiberglass to get a nice balance of the benefits of both.
