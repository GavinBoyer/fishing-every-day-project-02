# Weights

### Why So Heavy?

Weights and the amount of weight on your [line](fishing-line/index.md) plays a major factor in determining casting distance, sinking speed, and action in the water. There are many different weight types that each serve their own purposes. Without weights, we would need to constantly throw heavy [lures](artificial-baits.md) and [baits](organic-baits.md).

### Types of Weights

- **Bullet Weights**: These are the most common weight in bass fishing and is bullet shaped. They have a hole through the center where the line passes. Good for thick vegetation and soft plastics.
- **Egg Weights**: These have the same hole through the center, but are more of an egg shape. These are perfect for when you want free moving line while also keeping the line near the bottom.
- **Pyramid Weights**: Following the trend, these weights are shaped like a pyramid with an eye for the line at the top. These are excellent for fast moving, sandy bottom, water conditions.
- **Split-Shot**: These are the most common weight in angler's tackle boxes. It is a small, round weight with a slit in the center. To put it on the line, you put the line in the slit and squeeze the weight together. Good for ease of use and all water conditions.
- **Flat or Coin Weights**: These are disc shaped weights with an eye coming out of the edge. These are excellent for [float or bobber](floats-and-bobbers.md) fishing and excels at keeping your bait stationary.
- **Pencil Sinkers**: Much like the rest of the weights, this follows after the name and is a pencil shaped weight. This allows them to sink very quickly and are less likely to get caught in snags.
- **Drop-Shot Sinkers**: These are small egg shapes sinkers with a loop at the top for your line. They are easy to remove and dont require deconstruction of your entire rig. They sink very quickly and are usually rigged ==above== the hook.
- **Nail Weights**: These are the outliers of all the weights. Instead of tying these onto your line, you simply insert them into soft plastics. These are extremely versatile and can make your plastics much more realistic.

### When to Use

While each weight has its specific purpose, you can really use them interchangably. Especially egg sinkers, bullet weights, and drop shots. Weights tend to range in price and can get pretty expensive since the majority are made from lead.
