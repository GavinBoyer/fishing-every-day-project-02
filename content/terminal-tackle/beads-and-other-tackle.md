# Other Tackle and Equipment

### What Should be in Your Tackle Box

Every angler has some form of tackle box, whether it be the traditional cascading tiers, or several small organizational boxes in a backpack. Every angler has some method of storing and organizing their gear. Believe it or not, but you need more than just [hooks](hooks.md), [weights](weights.md), and [floats](floats-and-bobbers.md). 
- **An Assortment of Hooks**: Different hooks are meant for different species, having an assortment gives you the ability to target several species.
- **An Assortment of Weights**: Weights are responsible for getting your [bait](baits/index.md) lower into the water column, and no two bodies of water are the same depth. 
- **Bobbers and Floats**: You do not need a wide variety of floats. Just a few consistent ones that you know are good.
- **Basic [Baits](organic-baits.md) and [Lures](artificial-baits.md)**: Having a few worms, crickets, crankbaits, and soft plastics will give you the versatility to target essentially anything.
- **Leader Line**: Typically 12'-36' of [fluorocarbon](fluorocarbon.md) leader will be plenty of leader line. This is used to reduce the visibility of your line, particularly when using [braid](braid.md).
- **Needle Nose Pliers**: This might sound like a weird one, but it comes in handy for rigging split shots or getting [hooks](hooks.md) out of stubborn fish's mouths.
- **A Small Bait Knife**: This is really for when you are using cut bait, but you never know when a knife might be handy, so keeping one on you is always a good call.
- **Tape Measure and Scale**: If you are a catch and cook angler or like to log your catches, these are important tools in your box. A lot of keeper fish have a specific length they have to be, and without a tape measure, you would never know.
- **Line Cutters**: Cutting line with your teeth can easily damage them and pulling out a knife can be too difficult sometimes. Line cutters are a tiny pair of scissors specifically for cutting fishing line.
- **Other Miscellaneous Items**:
    - Bug spray
    - Sunscreen
    - Polarized sunglasses
    - First aid
    - Landing net
    - Camera

### The Tackle Box

Now that you have all of these items, you are ready to prepare and organize your tackle box however you choose. Remember, there is no correct or incorrect way to organize it. The only thing I would recommend is keeping saltwater gear seperate from freshwater gear.
