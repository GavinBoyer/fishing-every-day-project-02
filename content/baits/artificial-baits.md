# Artificial Baits

### What Exactly is Artificial Bait?

Artificial bait covers a wide variety of baits and lures. Some are as simple as a little piece of plastic or wood with some hooks, and some can be the nastiest smelling substance your nostrils have ever experienced.

When talking about artificial bait, it is important to mention that artificial lures are **not** a new invention. Anglers have been using artificial lures for thousands of years, with traces of them dating back to before the 17th century. It originated thousands of years ago when an angler tied feathers and some tree fiber onto a [hook](hooks.md) and cast it out. They gained more popularity in the 17th and 18th century which allowed lure makers to craft more sophisticated lures. When vulcanized rubber was invented, they unlocked even more possibilities. Fast forward to today and the average bass angler has anywhere from 5 to 30 lures in their tackle box.

In regards to artificial scents, there is evidence dating back to at least the 19th century of anglers utilizing artificial scents to troll for bass. Today, we have all sorts of scents and doughs ranging in smells and consistencies. 

#### Different Artificial Lures

Over the thousands of years since artificial lures first started being used, they have evolved immensely. To the point that there are specific lures for specific situations and water conditions.

![Different lure types](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0750/6608/1573/files/different_fishing_lures.webp?v=1695119087)

- **Jigs**: Jigs are a soft plastic-coated weight that sinks the lure and gives it a realistic jumping motion while working the rod. They excel in creating movement. They work well in both shallow and deep water and is best retrieved with a stop-and-go method. Jigs are best used on [spinning gear](spinning-reels.md).
- **Chatterbaits**: Chatterbaits are quite interesting and contain parts from several other baits to become an amalgamation of a lure. They are excellent for getting fish to quickly strike. When working a chatterbait, you want to be slow and intentional. Slowly retrieve it while throwing in a few rod pops. Chatterbaits tend to work better with a squid or craw trailer.

![Brown chatterbait](https://www.icastfishing.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/J_ChatterBaitEliteEVO_product_media_file_EXSCAFMM_res_web-2.jpg)
(Pictured: Chatterbait)

- **Crankbaits**: Crankbaits are extremely simple to use and are popular with beginners, yet they maintain the versatility for professionals. They rely on the speed you retrieve them. The faster the retrieve the faster the wiggle, the slower the retrieve, the slower and weaker the wiggle.
- **Jerkbaits**: Jerkbaits are a mid-water column lure that offers a little more speed than other lures. They appear in the water as a small fleeing bait fish and trigger predator fish to strike. They are normally a hard plastic body with 2-3 treble hooks, often with vibrant colors. Best fished with a stop-and-go method.

(Pictured: Jerkbait)

- **Swimbaits**: Swimbaits are one of the most unique and versatile lures. They can resemble a vast range of fish species, and often times, other animals like lizards or rodents. These baits are used to mimic a fish swimming through the water and can look quite realistic. They are offered in soft body, hard body, and paddle tail.

![Double-jointed rainbow trout swimbait](https://contents.mediadecathlon.com/p2901573/k$06043d2d40466a846f8703016b3f68ec/picture.jpg?format=auto&f=3000x0)
(Pictured: Swimbait)

- **Spinnerbaits**: Spinner baits are a wire L-shape with a jig head hook on the bottom and spinning blades on top. In the water these blades spin around and reflects light through the water to attract fish. They are great for predatory species and should be retrieved at a relatively quick pace.
![Maroon spinnerbait](https://cdn11.bigcommerce.com/s-palssl390t/images/stencil/1280x1280/products/141728/231550/strike-king-tgsb34ww-tour-grade-spinnerbait__48934.1697340648.jpg?c=1)
(Pictured: Spinnerbait)

- **Inline Spinners**: Inline spinners are the little brother of spinnerbaits. Instead of being an L-shape, the blade and the hook are on one straight piece of wire. These are often used for targeting trout. The blade creates a vibration and reflects light to mimic scales. 
- **Blades and Spoons**: Blades and spoons are also equally as capable at targeting predatory fish like pike, muskies, and salmon. Spoons are made to cut through heavy vegetation, so fishing those areas with spoons is a must. Blades and spoons are made to be reflective and mimic bait fish scales. This triggers predatory species to strike. Best retrieved in a slow steady motion.
- **Topwater Lures**: There are many types of topwater lures including, buzzbaits, poppers, frogs, and walk the dog lures. Topwaters are either going to be a constant retrieve or a stop-and-go retrieve depending on the type. Topwater lures are best suited for fishing in lily pads as they are meant to glide across the top, especially frogs. When using frogs it is best to use [braided line](braid.md) on a medium-heavy [baitcaster](baitcasting-rods).
- **Soft Plastic Baits**: Similarly to jigs, soft plastics are extremely versatile and allow for different types of fishing. Soft plastics can come in all shapes and sizes, from little green worms to massive pink lizards. It is best to let the plastic sink and give it a slight jerking motion before slowly reeling in the slack and repeating.

#### Artificial Scents and Doughs

Artificial scents and doughs are exactly what they sound like. They are made to mimic what the fish like to eat. There are several different kinds of both scents and doughs, and anglers will often use them in accordance with lures and live or cut [bait](organic-baits) to enhance the smell or appearance of the bait. Some popular artificial scents and doughs include:
- **Garlic Oil**
- **Fish Oil**
- **Catfish Charlie Dough**
- **Cherry Kool-Aid**
- **Small Scented Pellets**

Utilizing artificial scents and doughs can take your bite from zero to one hundred very quickly. While not guaranteed to work, it certainly helps your odds.
