# Bait

### What is Bait?

Bait is the organic or artificial material you put on your hook in order to attract the fish. This can range from a piece of fish all the way to a hard plastic crankbait. In this section we will cover:
- [Artificial Baits](artificial-baits.md)
- [Organic Baits](organic-baits.md)

To understand which kind of bait to use, you must analyze your target species eating habits. Some fish will jump all over an artificial lure while other fish don't even bat an eye, but will instantly attack a small worm on a [hook](hooks.md).

#### What Do Fish Eat?

Fish have a wide variety of diets, much like humans. some fish are herbivores, some are omnivores, and some are carnivores. Knowing what your target species is can help you significantly when choosing what bait to use. 

##### Herbivorous Fish

A lot of these fish won't even look at a piece of live or cut bait, but love vegetation and some lures. Some of them actually do not bite any thing and require you to spear fish or sight net them. For herbivores, use corn, and spoons to mimic vegetation. Some herbivorous fish include:
- Parrotfish
- Surgeonfish
- Plecostomus
- Certain Tilapia

##### Omnivorous Fish
These species will eat a mix of vegetation and meat. For omnivores, use live or cut bait, corn, and topwater lures for some catfish. Some omnivorous fish include:
- Catfish
- Goldfish
- Tilapia

##### Carnivorous Fish
Carnivorous fish will only eat meat and animal mimicking lures. For carnivorous fish, us live or cut bait, and [artificial lures](artificial-baits.md) such as topwaters, swimbaits, or soft plastics. Some carnivorous fish include:
- Sharks
- Bass
- Barracudas
- Tuna
