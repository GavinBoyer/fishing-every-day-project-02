# Organic Baits

### What is Organic Bait

Organic bait is classified as anything that naturally comes from the earth. This could be anything from worms to hot dogs. Organic is the more effective and efficient method of fishing. Using what the fish eat as your bait obviously attracts more fish than that of a hard plastic [lure](artificial-baits.md). This is especially effective for predatory fish like catfish and most saltwater fish.

#### What You Can Use as Bait

While yes, organic bait means bait that naturally occurs, it does not include several species. For instance, it is illegal to use game fish such as bass, trout, or catfish. You also cannot transport live bait from one water source to another. What you catch in one place must stay there. Anglers use both live and cut bait. While some species do require using one or the other, it is mostly up to preference. Some popular baits are:
- **Panfish** (bluegill, green sunfish, etc.)
- **Worms** (nightcrawlers and red worms are most popular)
- **Grass Carp** (mostly used cut up for alligator gar)
- **Hot Dogs** (catfish love them, especially with some [scent](artificial-baits.md) on them)
- **Shad**
- **Mullet**
- **Shrimp**
- **Crab**
- **Goldfish**
- **Minnows and Creek Chubs**
- **Squid**
- **Crawfish**
- **Chicken Livers**
- **Corn**

#### Which Bait to Pick

The only thing you really need to analyze when picking a bait is what your target species eats. If they are bottom feeders like carp, then use corn. If they are a big predator fish, use bluegill or shad. Studying your target fish and knowing their eating habits can increase your hook up chances. The bigger the bait you pick, the larger the [hook](hooks.md) and the stronger the [line](fishing-line) must be.
