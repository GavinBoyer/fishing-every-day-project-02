# Fishing Every Day

As an avid angler and outdoorsman, I feel it is my duty to educate my fellow and future angler's on the basics of fishing and the equipment required. Within this knowledge base you will find all the information you need to start fishing. From what [rod](rods/index.md) to pick all the way down to the [bait](baits/index.md) you put on the [hook](hooks.md), it is in this site. 

Whether you are a beginner or seasoned veteran angler, there is bound to be some information in this site that you can find of use. Between my extensive fishing experience and my research on the internet, I believe I am providing an above-adequate database for all things fishing. 

Sometimes fishing is easy and fun, and other times it can be extremely frustrating and challenging. My goal with this site is to help make some of those frustrating times into enjoyable times. Fishing mad is no fun at all.
