## Fishing Line

When picking what fishing line to use, it is important to consider the many different factors and pieces of equipment needed for each type. Fishing line comes in all shapes and sizes, from near invisible [monofilament](monofilament.md) to 150lbs [braid](braid.md), there is something that fits your needs.

### Different Types of Fishing Line

There are several different types of fishing line, and each has its own pros and cons. Some are excellent for reeling in true giants, but would not get a bite from a slightly smaller, more keen fish. 
- [Monofilament](monofilament.md)
- [Fluorocarbon](fluorocarbon.md)
- [Copolymer](copolymer.md)
- [Braid](braid.md)
- [Fly Line](fly-line.md)

While each line is excellent at what it does, use it in the wrong way and you will find yourself in a world of problems.

#### What Factors to Determine

There are several key factors you need to analyze before you pick your line.
- **What [[reels|reel]] are you using**?
- **How much line do you need?
- **What species are you targeting?
- **Where are you fishing**?
After reading about each line and answering these questions, you can determine what line is best for you.
